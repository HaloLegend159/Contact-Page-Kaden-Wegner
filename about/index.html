Background

Aviation has been a constant thread throughout my life. I'm currently working toward my Private Pilot Certificate — a goal that grew naturally from years of hands-on work in and around aircraft. That passion for flight led me to enlist as a Chinook Helicopter Mechanic (15-U) with the Nebraska Army National Guard, where I work on one of the most capable heavy-lift helicopters in the U.S. military.

I'm Kaden Wegner, co-founder of SkyGUARD. Growing up with a hands-on technical background — from aircraft systems to software development — I've always been drawn to solving real problems with precision tools.

My time in the Guard has taught me that documentation isn't optional. In aviation maintenance, a record that's unclear or incomplete can have real consequences. I brought that same standard to roof inspections — every photo labeled, every finding summarized, every deliverable shareable and professionally structured.

I'm also a co-owner of Service Affiliates Nebraska, focused on property damage documentation and contracting. That experience working directly with contractors and adjusters showed me a gap: the industry needed faster, safer, and more consistent aerial documentation. SkyGUARD is my answer to that gap.
